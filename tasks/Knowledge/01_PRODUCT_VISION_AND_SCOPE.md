# Product Vision And Scope

## Vision

KnowledgeOS becomes the trusted memory layer of StreamlineOS. It should replace scattered notes, PDFs, SOPs, policies, help articles, onboarding guides, chat answers, and folder-based documents with one governed, searchable, AI-ready knowledge system.

## Audiences

- Individual users writing private notes.
- Teams documenting SOPs and decisions.
- Admins publishing policies and handbooks.
- Support teams maintaining customer-facing help articles.
- Product and engineering teams maintaining runbooks and technical docs.
- AI assistants answering questions using permission-safe knowledge.

## In Scope

- Spaces and collections.
- Articles/pages/notes.
- Nested page tree.
- Rich editor.
- File uploads.
- Document parsing.
- Version history.
- Comments and mentions.
- Sharing and permissions.
- Templates.
- Approvals.
- Verification/freshness.
- Search.
- RAG AI Ask.
- Citations.
- Public help center.
- Analytics.
- Audit logs.
- Import/export.
- Trash/restore.

## Out Of Scope

- Core authentication.
- Core billing engine.
- File storage provider infrastructure.
- Chat realtime transport.
- Full support ticket lifecycle.

## Success Metrics

- Search success rate.
- AI answer helpfulness.
- Citation accuracy.
- Reduction in repeated questions.
- Support deflection.
- Article freshness compliance.
- Time to find answer.
- Monthly active readers/authors.
