# Knowledge Gaps And Feedback

## Gap Sources

- Search with zero results.
- AI no-context answer.
- Repeated chat questions.
- Support tickets without article.
- Negative answer feedback.
- Expired verification.
- Contradictory sources.

- Helpful.
- Not helpful.
- Wrong answer.
- Bad citation.
- Missing detail.
