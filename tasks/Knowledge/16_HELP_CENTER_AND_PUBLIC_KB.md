# Help Center And Public KB

## Public Help Center Features

- Public article list.
- Categories/spaces.
- Article detail.
- Search.
- Related articles.
- Feedback.
- SEO metadata.
- Public AI Ask optional.

## Public AI Rules

- Use only public published articles.
- Never use internal/private/team docs.
- Rate limit anonymous users.
- No hidden citation metadata.
- No account-specific answers unless authenticated and permissioned.

## Support Integration

- Link support tickets to articles.
- Suggest articles while replying.
- Create article from resolved ticket.
- Track deflection.
- Track negative feedback.

## SEO

- Public slug.
- SEO title.
- SEO description.
- Canonical URL.
- Sitemap inclusion.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Public help center cannot leak internal docs.
- Support agents can reuse internal and public KB appropriately.
