# StreamlineOS Product Bible

# Billing, Pricing & Marketplace Platform

# 06_Checkout_Experience.md

## Purpose
Design a frictionless checkout that supports self-service and enterprise purchases.

## Checkout Flow
1. Select Platform
2. Select Apps/Bundles
3. Choose Seats
4. Choose AI Credits
5. Review Pricing
6. Tax Calculation
7. Payment
8. Confirmation

## UX
- Live price updates
- Annual/monthly toggle
- Coupon support
- Clear savings
- Trust badges

## Acceptance Criteria
Checkout completion optimized for conversion.
