- Currency.
- Billing mode.

- Retainer.

- Actual cost.

## Reports

- Burn rate.

- Margin.
- Invoice readiness.

## Edge Cases

- Time logged to closed task.
- User edits approved time.
- Budget exceeded.
- Currency mismatch.
- Client sees internal cost.
