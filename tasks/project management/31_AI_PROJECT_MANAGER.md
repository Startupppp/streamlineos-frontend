# AI Project Manager

## AI Capabilities

- Create project plan from prompt.
- Break milestones into tasks.
- Suggest assignees.
- Estimate complexity.
- Predict delivery date.
- Detect risk.
- Summarize project.
- Summarize sprint.
- Draft client update.
- Convert meeting notes to tasks.
- Convert chat to tasks.
- Explain delay.

## AI Questions

- What should I do today?
- What is blocked?
- Why is this project late?
- What changed since yesterday?
- What is at risk this week?
- Who is overloaded?

## Guardrails

- AI cannot delete data.
- AI cannot approve client/financial changes.
- AI suggestions must show evidence.
- AI actions create audit trail.

## Edge Cases

- AI suggests unavailable assignee.
- AI summarizes client-hidden data into client report.
- AI creates duplicate tasks.
- AI uses stale project state.
