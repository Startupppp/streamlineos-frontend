# Product Vision And Positioning

## Vision

ProjectOS is the command center for planning, executing, testing, delivering, and reporting work. It should help teams create projects from templates, assign clear roles, run sprints, track bugs, manage clients, automate repetitive work, and understand project health without spreadsheets or disconnected chat.

## Product Promise

Create a project, configure how the team works, execute with tasks and boards, collaborate in chat, test and release with discipline, report progress clearly, and keep business controls like budget, time, approvals, and client scope in one place.

## Target Buyers

- Founders and owners who need execution visibility.
- Project managers who need workflow control.
- Engineering teams who need sprints, QA, bugs, and releases.
- Agencies and service companies who need client portals, change requests, time, and billing.
- Operations teams who need recurring work, approvals, and automation.

## Core Differentiator

ProjectOS should be chat-connected and configuration-first. Every project can create its own chat channel, workflow, templates, forms, dashboards, and automation pack.

## Non Goals

- Do not build a generic to-do list only.
- Do not force every team into Scrum.
- Do not expose internal work to clients by default.
- Do not make configuration so complex that setup becomes painful.
- Do not include whiteboard.
