# Inventory & Warehouse Product Bible

# 001 Vision & Goals

## Purpose
Build an enterprise-grade Inventory & Warehouse Management System (WMS) supporting multi-company, multi-warehouse operations.

## Objectives
- Real-time inventory visibility
- End-to-end stock lifecycle
- Barcode-first operations
- AI-assisted replenishment
- Full auditability
- Multi-tenant architecture

## Success Metrics
- Inventory accuracy >99%
- Reduced stockouts
- Faster fulfillment
- Lower carrying costs
