# Org Structure, Departments, Teams And Locations

## Features
- Departments
- Teams
- Branches/locations
- Cost centers
- Job roles
- Job levels
- Reporting lines
- Matrix managers
- Org chart
- Position management
- Headcount planning

## Org Changes
Support effective-dated:
- Department transfer
- Manager change
- Location transfer
- Designation change
- Job level change

## Acceptance Criteria
- Org chart uses effective current reporting data.
- Historical org changes are preserved.
- Headcount by department/location/role is reportable.
