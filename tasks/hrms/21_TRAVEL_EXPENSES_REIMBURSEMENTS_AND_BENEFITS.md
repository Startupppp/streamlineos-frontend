# Travel, Expenses, Reimbursements And Benefits

## Features
- Travel request
- Field visit tracking
- Expense claims
- Reimbursements
- Medical claims
- Insurance benefits
- Dependents
- Salary advances
- Employee loans
- Payroll deductions

## Acceptance Criteria
- Reimbursement approvals use workflow engine.
- Benefits eligibility is policy-driven.
- Payroll deductions integrate with payroll.
- Approved reimbursements can be marked as payroll-payable, finance-payable, or already-paid.
- Salary advances, employee loans, medical claims, benefits, travel claims, and deductions must sync to payroll with effective period and approval status.
