# AI HR Copilot And Policy Q&A

## Features
- Ask HR policy questions.
- Employee risk summary.
- Attendance anomaly explanation.
- Performance review draft.
- Promotion letter draft.
- Attrition risk signals.
- Skill gap suggestions.
- Workforce planning assistant.

## Safety
- AI must respect permissions.
- AI must cite handbook/policy sources.
- AI cannot expose salary/grievance/sensitive data without permission.

## Acceptance Criteria
- AI disabled mode works.
- AI answers cite sources.
- AI suggestions require human approval for official actions.
