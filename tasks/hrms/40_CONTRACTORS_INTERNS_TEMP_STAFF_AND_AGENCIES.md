# Contractors, Interns, Temp Staff And Agencies

## Goal
Support non-standard workers.

## Worker Types
- Full-time employee
- Part-time employee
- Contractor
- Consultant
- Intern
- Temporary staff
- Agency staff
- Freelancer

## Features
- Contract start/end
- Renewal reminders
- Agency/vendor mapping
- Timesheet-based payment
- Stipend
- Internship certificate
- Conversion to employee
- Rehire eligibility
- Access expiry

## Acceptance Criteria
- Policies can differ by worker type.
- Contract end triggers offboarding/access removal workflow.
- Intern can be converted to employee without duplicate record.
