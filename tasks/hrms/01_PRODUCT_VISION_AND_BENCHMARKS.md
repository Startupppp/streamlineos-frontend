# Product Vision And Benchmarks

## Vision
Build HRMS as a configurable PeopleOS: one system for employees, policies, workflows, automations, documents, attendance, leaves, payroll handoff, performance, learning, engagement, compliance, and HR analytics.

## Benchmark Lessons
- Odoo HR shows the value of connected HR apps: employees, time off, recruitment, appraisals, approvals, documents, referrals, signatures, and payroll-adjacent workflows.
- Zoho People shows strong configurability across leave, attendance, shift scheduling, timesheets, performance, onboarding, mobile self-service, and HR workflows.
- BambooHR shows clean employee records, onboarding, reports, e-signatures, performance, payroll integrations, and HR assistant workflows.
- Rippling shows the bigger opportunity: HR, IT, devices, apps, payroll, and finance actions automated from employee lifecycle events.

## Product Positioning
"A configurable HR operating system where every HR process is policy-driven, workflow-driven, template-driven, and automation-ready."

## Target Personas
- Employee
- Manager
- HR admin
- Payroll admin
- Finance admin
- IT/admin ops
- Founder/CEO
- Compliance/legal owner

## Success Metrics
- New employee onboarding configured in under 30 minutes.
- Leave policy configured without code.
- Attendance rule configured without code.
- Approval workflow configured without code.
- HR admin can change letter templates without developer help.
- Every sensitive field change has audit trail.
- Employee can complete common self-service requests without HR help.
