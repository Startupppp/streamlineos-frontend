# Offboarding, Exit, FNF And Alumni

## Features
- Resignation
- Termination
- Notice period
- Exit checklist
- Asset recovery
- Access removal
- Knowledge transfer
- Exit interview
- FNF calculation handoff
- Relieving letter
- Experience letter
- Alumni record
- Rehire eligibility

## Acceptance Criteria
- Offboarding tasks are template-driven.
- Notice period is policy-driven.
- Asset and access tasks are automatically created.
- Former employee remains alumni, not deleted.
