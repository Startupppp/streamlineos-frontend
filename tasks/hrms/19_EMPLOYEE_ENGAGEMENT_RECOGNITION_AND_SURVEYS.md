# Employee Engagement, Recognition And Surveys

## Features
- Pulse surveys
- eNPS
- Mood check-ins
- Anonymous feedback
- Kudos
- Badges
- Reward points
- Employee of the month
- Announcements
- Birthdays and anniversaries
- Culture calendar

## Acceptance Criteria
- Surveys can be anonymous.
- Engagement analytics are aggregated safely.
- Recognition appears on employee timeline.
