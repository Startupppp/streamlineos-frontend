# Health, Safety, Incidents And Wellness

## Goal
Support employee safety and wellbeing.

## Features
- Workplace incident reports
- Injury/accident logs
- Safety training
- Wellness check-ins
- Burnout signals
- Sick leave patterns
- HR intervention workflow
- Emergency contacts
- Medical document privacy

## Acceptance Criteria
- Health/safety cases are confidential.
- Incidents can trigger workflow and notifications.
- Medical data has strict field-level permissions.
