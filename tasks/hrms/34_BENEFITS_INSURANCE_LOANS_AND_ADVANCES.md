# Benefits, Insurance, Loans And Advances

## Goal
Handle employee benefits and financial support workflows.

## Features
- Benefit plans
- Eligibility rules
- Enrollment windows
- Dependents
- Insurance policy details
- Claims
- Salary advances
- Employee loans
- Repayment schedules
- Payroll deductions
- Benefit documents

## Backend Requirements
- Effective-dated benefits.
- Eligibility policy engine.
- Payroll deduction contract.
- Approval workflow for advances/loans/claims.

## Frontend Requirements
- My benefits.
- HR benefits admin.
- Claims dashboard.
- Loan/advance request.
- Repayment schedule view.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Benefits eligibility is policy-driven.
- Approved deductions flow to payroll.
- Employee can see active benefits and dependents.
