# HRMS Product Bible

# 001 Vision & Goals

## Purpose
Build an enterprise Human Resource Management System for the complete employee lifecycle.

## Objectives
- Employee lifecycle management
- Recruitment to offboarding
- Attendance & Leave
- Payroll integrations
- Performance management
- Learning & Development
- Asset management
- Enterprise RBAC
- Multi-tenant architecture

## Success Metrics
- Reduced HR administration time
- Automated approvals
- Accurate attendance
- Compliance-ready audit trails
- Self-service employee portal
