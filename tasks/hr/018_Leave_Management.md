# 018 Leave Management

## Features
- Apply, edit, cancel leave
- Half-day & hourly leave
- Multi-level approvals
- Balance tracking
- Leave calendar
- Team availability
- Attachments
- Delegation during leave

Annual, Sick, Casual, Maternity, Paternity, Bereavement, Unpaid, Custom.
