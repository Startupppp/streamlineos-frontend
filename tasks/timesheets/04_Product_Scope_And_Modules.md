# StreamlineOS Product Bible

# Timesheets / Worklogs

# 04_Product_Scope_And_Modules.md

## Product Modules

### 1. My Time

For employees and individual contributors.

Includes:

- today timer,
- weekly timesheet grid,
- manual entry,
- recent projects/tasks,
- draft/submitted/approved/rejected status,
- missing log reminders,
- rejected entry corrections.

### 2. Team Time

For managers and project leads.

Includes:

- team weekly overview,
- submission status,
- missing logs,
- approval queue,
- workload/utilization,
- overtime alerts,
- project/time distribution.

### 3. Approvals

For managers, project managers, finance, and client approvers.

Includes:

- internal approvals,
- client approvals,
- bulk approve/reject,
- comments,
- delegation,
- approval history,
- period locks.

### 4. Billing Queue

For finance and owners.

Includes:

- approved billable hours,
- uninvoiced hours,
- rate resolution,
- invoice draft generation,
- retainer/hour bank consumption,
- export to accounting/billing.

### 5. Payroll Queue

For HR/finance.

Includes:

- approved payable hours,
- overtime,
- shift-aware hours,
- breaks,
- export CSV/XLSX,
- payroll system mapping.

### 6. Reports

For owners and managers.

Includes:

- utilization,
- billable ratio,
- project budget burn,
- client profitability,
- employee timesheet compliance,
- approval SLA,
- overtime,
- missing logs,
- invoice leakage.

### 7. Settings

For admins.

Includes:

- work week,
- rounding rules,
- time entry restrictions,
- required fields,
- approval rules,
- lock periods,
- billable defaults,
- rate cards,
- reminder rules,
- integrations,
- custom fields.

### 8. Client Portal

For customer approval.

Includes:

- customer-visible approved work summary,
- comments,
- approve/reject,
- downloadable PDF/CSV,
- invoice preview.

## MVP Scope

Must include:

- weekly timesheet grid,
- manual time entry,
- timer,
- submit weekly timesheet,
- manager approval/rejection,
- billable/non-billable,
- project/task/client fields,
- basic rates,
- billing export,
- reports dashboard,
- settings for required fields and rounding,
- audit log.

## V1 Scope

Add:

- calendar view,
- client approvals,
- invoice draft generation,
- payroll export,
- budgets,
- reminders,
- mobile responsive capture,
- imports,
- integrations,
- advanced reports.

## V2 Scope

Add:

- offline mobile,
- kiosk mode,
- auto-tracker,
- screenshots/app tracking optional and disabled by default,
- AI time suggestions,
- forecasting,
- resource planning,
- deep accounting integrations.

## Out Of Scope For MVP

- invasive employee monitoring by default,
- screenshots by default,
- keystroke tracking,
- payroll processing,
- tax filing,
- full project management replacement,
- full accounting replacement.

## Product Principles

- Fast for employees.
- Strict enough for finance.
- Flexible enough for agencies.
- Transparent enough for clients.
- Auditable enough for compliance.
- Standalone enough to sell alone.
- Integrated enough to expand into StreamlineOS.
