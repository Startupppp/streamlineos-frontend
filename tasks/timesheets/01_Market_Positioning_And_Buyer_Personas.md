# Timesheets / Worklogs

# 01_Market_Positioning_And_Buyer_Personas.md

## Positioning

TimeFlow helps service teams turn work hours into approved payroll, accurate invoices, and clear project profitability.

Primary tagline:

> Track time. Approve work. Bill accurately.

Alternative sales lines:

- Stop losing billable hours.
- Know exactly where team time goes.
- Turn approved worklogs into invoices.
- Make payroll and client billing painless.
- One timesheet product for projects, clients, teams, and finance.

## Why This Is Easy To Sell

Timesheets sell because the pain is obvious:

- teams forget to log work,
- managers chase people every Friday,
- clients dispute invoices,
- projects silently overrun budgets,
- payroll needs clean hours,
- finance manually consolidates spreadsheets,
- founders do not know which clients/projects are profitable.

## Ideal Customer Profiles

### 1. Agencies

Examples:

- marketing agencies,
- design studios,
- development agencies,
- SEO/content agencies,
- creative studios.

Pain:

- underbilling,
- retainers overused,
- unclear team utilization,
- client questions about hours.

Best pitch:

- "Know which clients make money and invoice every approved hour."

### 2. IT Services And Consultancies

Pain:

- engineers forget time logs,
- multiple client projects,
- support retainers,
- T&M billing,
- project budget overruns.

Best pitch:

- "Track work against clients, tickets, and project budgets without spreadsheet chaos."

### 3. Professional Services

Examples:

- accounting firms,
- legal offices,
- consulting firms,
- architecture/interior design.

Pain:

- billable hour leakage,
- client approval,
- multiple billing rates,
- retainers.

Best pitch:

- "Turn timesheets into clean client bills and profitability reports."

### 4. Construction And Field Services

Pain:

- site-wise attendance/worklogs,
- mobile/offline logging,
- crew hours,
- overtime,
- supervisor approval.

Best pitch:

- "Capture field hours from mobile and approve them before payroll."

### 5. SMB Internal Teams

Pain:

- employee productivity visibility,
- project costing,
- payroll hours,
- overtime.

Best pitch:

- "Simple weekly timesheets with approvals, reminders, and reports."

## Buyer Personas

### Founder / Owner

Cares about:

- revenue leakage,
- project profitability,
- employee productivity,
- client disputes.

Must see:

- billable vs non-billable,
- client profitability,
- team utilization,
- overdue approvals,
- missed time logs.

### Operations Manager

Cares about:

- clean process,
- reminders,
- approvals,
- exceptions.

Must see:

- submitted/pending/rejected sheets,
- missing logs,
- period locks,
- audit trail.

### Project Manager

Cares about:

- task/project budget,
- team effort,
- delivery velocity,
- client reporting.

Must see:

- hours by project/task,
- budget burn,
- approvals,
- comments and evidence links.

### Finance/Admin

Cares about:

- billing,
- payroll,
- rates,
- exports,
- tax/invoice support.

Must see:

- approved billable hours,
- uninvoiced hours,
- payroll export,
- rate cards,
- invoice drafts.

### Employee

Cares about:

- fast logging,
- no duplicate entry,
- mobile support,
- clear status.

Must see:

- today timer,
- weekly grid,
- recently used projects/tasks,
- draft/submitted/approved/rejected state.

## Product Promise

The product should make the employee experience fast and the manager/finance experience controlled.

Employee promise:

- log time in seconds.

Manager promise:

- approve clean weekly timesheets without chasing.

Finance promise:

- bill/pay only approved hours.

Owner promise:

- know what work is profitable.
