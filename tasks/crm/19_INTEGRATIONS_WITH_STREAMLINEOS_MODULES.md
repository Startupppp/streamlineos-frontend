# Integrations With StreamlineOS Modules

## Integrations
- Notifications: assignments, SLA, approvals, replies.
- Calendar: meetings, reminders, tasks.
- Chat: mention CRM records, create lead from chat.
- Knowledge: sales playbooks, AI answers.
- Sign: quote/proposal signing.
- Accounting-finance: invoice/payment/account ledger.
- Payments: payment links.
- Projects: won deal to project/onboarding.
- Helpdesk: customer tickets in Customer 360.
- Surveys: NPS/CSAT and lead forms.
- Inventory: products, availability, quotes.
- HRMS: sales team capacity/territory ownership.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Customer 360 shows cross-module data with permissions.
- Won deal can trigger downstream workflows.
- Quote-to-cash integration is traceable.
