# Product Strategy And Benchmarks

## Vision
CRM should become the revenue operating system of StreamlineOS: lead capture, qualification, routing, nurturing, pipeline, deals, quotes, approvals, signatures, invoicing, payments, onboarding, projects, support, renewals, and expansion in one connected workflow.

## Benchmark Lessons
- Salesforce: unified customer platform, AI agents, ecosystem, deep customization.
- HubSpot: approachable automation, workflows, lead scoring, forms, email nurture, lifecycle marketing.
- Zoho CRM: workflow rules, assignment rules, Blueprint process enforcement, AI/Zia, strong customization.
- Pipedrive: simple visual pipeline, lead generation tools, chatbot/live chat/web forms/scheduler, sales automation.

## Better-Than-Them Direction
- Keep Pipedrive simplicity.
- Add HubSpot-style journey automation.
- Add Zoho-style process blueprint.
- Add Salesforce-style Customer 360 and AI agents.
- Make setup far faster and less bloated than enterprise CRM.

## Core Revenue Flow
Lead capture -> enrichment -> scoring -> routing -> SLA -> sequence -> activity -> deal -> approval -> quote -> sign -> invoice -> payment -> onboarding/project -> support -> renewal/upsell.
