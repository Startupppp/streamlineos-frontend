# Leads: Capture, Routing, Scoring And Dedupe

## Capture Channels
- Manual entry
- CSV/XLSX import
- Web forms
- Public contact forms
- Chatbot
- Live chat
- WhatsApp
- Email parser
- API
- Social leads
- Campaign/UTM
- Referral
- Partner

## Routing
Support:
- round robin
- weighted round robin
- least loaded
- territory
- product interest
- source
- language
- availability
- time zone
- VIP account
- skill-based

## Scoring
Score dimensions:
- fit score
- intent score
- engagement score
- source score
- AI score
- negative score

## Dedupe
Match on:
- email
- phone
- company/domain
- WhatsApp number
- fuzzy name/company
- external source ID

## Acceptance Criteria
- Lead source/priorities are configurable.
- Routing has preview/test mode.
- Scoring rules are auditable.
- Duplicate merge preserves timeline.
