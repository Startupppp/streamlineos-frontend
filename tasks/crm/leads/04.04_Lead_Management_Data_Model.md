# Lead Management - Part 04 Data Model

## Core Entities
- Lead
- LeadOwner
- LeadSource
- LeadActivity
- LeadTimeline
- LeadTag
- LeadCustomField
- LeadAttachment

## Lead Fields
- id (UUID)
- organization_id
- owner_id
- first_name
- last_name
- company
- email
- phone
- status
- score
- source
- priority
- expected_value
- created_at
- updated_at
- archived_at

## Relationships
Lead -> Activities (1:N)
Lead -> Notes (1:N)
Lead -> Attachments (1:N)
Lead -> Tasks (1:N)

## Rules
- Multi-tenant isolation
- Soft delete
- Audit enabled
- Indexed email, phone, owner, status
