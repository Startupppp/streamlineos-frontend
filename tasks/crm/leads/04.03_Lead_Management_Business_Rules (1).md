# CRM Product Bible

# Lead Management
## Part 03 - Business Rules

### Validation
- Email optional but unique when present
- Phone normalized
- Owner required
- Status mandatory

### Assignment
- Manual
- Round Robin
- Territory
- Workload based
- AI recommendation

### Duplicate Rules
Match by:
- Email
- Phone
- Company + Name

### Conversion
Lead -> Contact + Company + Deal

### Audit
Track every create, edit, merge, assignment, conversion and delete event.

### Permissions
Sales Rep:
Own leads.

Manager:
Team leads.

Admin:
All leads.

### Automation
- Auto assignment
- Follow-up reminders
- SLA timers
- Lead aging
