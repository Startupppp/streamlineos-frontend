# CRM Product Bible

# Lead Management
## Part 01 - Product Overview

### Vision
Build an enterprise-grade Lead Management system that captures, qualifies, assigns, nurtures and converts prospects into customers.

### Goals
- Central lead repository
- Multi-source lead capture
- AI-assisted qualification
- Complete audit trail
- Enterprise RBAC
- Multi-tenant architecture

### Personas
- Sales Representative
- Sales Manager
- SDR
- Marketing
- Administrator

### Lead Lifecycle
New → Assigned → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost

### Core Capabilities
- Create/Edit/Delete
- Archive/Restore
- Merge
- Duplicate Detection
- Assignment
- Activities
- Timeline
- Files
- Notes
- Tags
- Custom Fields
- Import/Export
- Conversion
- Automation
- Notifications
- Analytics

### Acceptance Criteria
Complete lead lifecycle supported with full auditing.
