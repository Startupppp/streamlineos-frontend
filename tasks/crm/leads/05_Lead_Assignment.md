# CRM Product Bible
# 05 Lead Assignment

## Purpose
Automatically and manually assign leads to the right owner.

## Assignment Methods
- Manual
- Round Robin
- Territory
- Team Queue
- Workload Based
- AI Recommendation

## Rules
- Respect business hours
- Skip inactive users
- Reassign orphaned leads
- Audit every assignment

## UI
Assignment dialog, bulk assignment, queue monitor.

## KPIs
Assignment SLA, response time, reassignment rate.
