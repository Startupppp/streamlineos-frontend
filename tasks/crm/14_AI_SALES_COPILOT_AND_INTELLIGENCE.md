# AI Sales Copilot And Intelligence

## AI Features
- lead summary
- deal summary
- next best action
- email draft
- call summary
- meeting notes
- objection handling
- close probability
- churn/renewal risk
- duplicate suggestions
- workflow suggestions
- report creation

## Safety
- AI respects permissions.
- AI suggestions require user approval for official sends/updates.
- AI explains score and confidence.

## Acceptance Criteria
- AI can be disabled by org.
- AI output is not blindly written to CRM records.
- AI actions are audited.
