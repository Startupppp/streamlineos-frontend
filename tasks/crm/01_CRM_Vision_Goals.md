# CRM Platform
# 01_CRM_Vision_Goals

## Purpose
Build an enterprise CRM for managing the complete customer lifecycle.

## Objectives
- Lead-to-Customer lifecycle
- AI-first CRM
- Multi-tenant
- Enterprise RBAC
- Automation ready

## Core Modules
- Leads
- Contacts
- Companies
- Deals
- Activities
- Tasks
- Reports
- AI

## Success Metrics
- Faster lead response
- Higher conversion
- Complete activity tracking
- Accurate forecasting

## Acceptance Criteria
Production-ready, scalable, auditable.
