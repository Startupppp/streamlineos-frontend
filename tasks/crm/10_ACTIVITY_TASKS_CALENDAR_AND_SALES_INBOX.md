# Activities, Tasks, Calendar And Sales Inbox

## Activity Types
Must be configurable:
- call
- email
- meeting
- note
- WhatsApp
- LinkedIn
- demo
- proposal review
- custom

## Sales Inbox
One place for:
- due tasks
- overdue follow-ups
- new replies
- meetings today
- SLA risk leads
- stuck deals
- assigned leads
- AI recommended actions

## Calendar
Integrate:
- CRM meetings
- follow-ups
- demos
- renewal reminders
- quote expiry reminders

## Acceptance Criteria
- Activity types are metadata-driven.
- No deal/lead can be stale without surfacing in inbox.
- Calendar items deep-link to CRM records.
