# Quotes, Products, Pricebooks And Quote-To-Cash

## Features
- Product catalog
- Price books
- Discounts
- Taxes
- Quote templates
- Quote approvals
- Send quote
- E-sign
- Convert to invoice
- Payment link
- Sync to accounting

## Validation
- Discount thresholds require approval.
- Quote cannot be sent without valid contact/account.
- Quote expiry date required.
- Product price must come from active pricebook unless override allowed.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Quote-to-cash path is traceable.
- Quote templates are configurable.
- Signed quote can create invoice/project/onboarding.
