# Attendance LOP Overtime And Timesheet Inputs

## Purpose

Use attendance, leave, overtime, and timesheets in payroll.

## Inputs

- Paid days.
- LOP days.
- Half days.
- Overtime hours.
- Overtime days.
- Shift allowances.
- Holiday work.
- Timesheet billable hours.

## Source Systems

- Attendance module.
- Leave module.
- Timesheets module.
- Manual upload.
- Manual adjustment.

## Review UI

Show:

- Employee.
- Scheduled days.
- Paid days.
- LOP.
- Overtime.
- Source.
- Warning.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Payroll admin can override with reason.
- Source data is visible.
- Overtime calculation respects policy toggles.
