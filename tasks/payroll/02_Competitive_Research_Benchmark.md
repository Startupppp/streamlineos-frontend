# Competitive Research Benchmark

## Gusto Lessons

Strengths:

- Simple owner-friendly setup.
- Automated payroll calculations.
- Tax filing support.
- Benefits and HR integration.
- Employee self-service.

PayrollOS takeaway:

- Setup must be understandable to non-payroll experts.
- Employee self-service is not optional.

## Rippling Lessons

Strengths:

- Unified employee data.
- Payroll changes explained across runs.
- Automation and approvals.
- Global workforce support.

PayrollOS takeaway:

- Payroll must compare current run with previous run.
- Employee master data should drive payroll automatically.

## ADP Lessons

Strengths:

- Direct deposit.
- Tax deductions and compliance.
- Time and attendance integration.
- Mobile payroll.

PayrollOS takeaway:

- Bank payout, deductions, compliance, and attendance integration are core.

## Deel Lessons

Strengths:

- Global payroll.
- Contractors.
- EOR and compliance.
- Multi-country workforce.

PayrollOS takeaway:

- Contractor and global templates must be first-class, even if advanced compliance is phased.

## QuickBooks / Xero Lessons

Strengths:

- Accounting alignment.
- Journal exports.
- Reports.

PayrollOS takeaway:

- Payroll must produce finance-ready reports, not just payslips.

## Product Differentiation

PayrollOS should win by combining:

- Template-first payroll setup.
- Toggle-driven configuration.
- Monthly command center.
- Exception-first review.
- Beautiful employee self-service.
- Payroll variance explanations.
- StreamlineOS integrations with attendance, timesheets, recruitment, onboarding, expenses, accounting, and documents.
