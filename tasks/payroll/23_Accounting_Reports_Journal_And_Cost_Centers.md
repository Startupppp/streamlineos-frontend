# Accounting Reports Journal And Cost Centers

## Purpose

Make payroll finance-ready.

## Reports

- Payroll summary.
- Employee payroll register.
- Department payroll cost.
- Cost center report.
- Earnings report.
- Deduction report.
- Reimbursement report.
- Tax report.
- Bank payout report.
- Variance report.

## Accounting Export

Support:

- Journal entry export.
- CSV export.
- Cost center mapping.
- Component-to-ledger mapping.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Reports can filter by month, department, location, cost center, employee type.
- Finance can export payroll journal.
- Reports match locked payroll run.
