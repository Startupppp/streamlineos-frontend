# Full And Final Settlement

## Purpose

Handle employee exit payouts.

## FNF Components

- Pending salary.
- Leave encashment.
- Bonus due.
- Reimbursements.
- Loan recovery.
- Asset recovery deductions.
- Notice period recovery.
- Other deductions.
- Net payable.

## Workflow

1. Resignation/offboarding trigger.
2. Pull dues and recoveries.
3. HR review.
4. Finance review.
5. Approval.
6. Payment.
7. FNF payslip/statement.

## Acceptance Criteria

- FNF is separate from regular payroll but can reference payroll data.
- Settlement components are auditable.
- Employee can download FNF statement if enabled.
