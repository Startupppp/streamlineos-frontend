# Reimbursements Expenses And Claims

## Purpose

Bring approved reimbursements into payroll.

## Claim Types

- Travel.
- Food.
- Internet.
- Medical.
- Fuel.
- Office supplies.
- Client project expenses.
- Custom category.

## Workflow

1. Employee submits claim.
2. Manager/finance approves.
3. Approved claim enters payroll input.
4. Payroll admin reviews.
5. Reimbursement appears on payslip if configured.

## Controls

- Category budget.
- Receipt required.
- Taxable/non-taxable.
- Payroll month.
- Approval chain.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Pending/rejected claims do not enter payroll.
- Paid claims cannot be paid again.
- Reimbursements can be reported separately from salary.
