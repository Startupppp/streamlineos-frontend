# Payslip Designer Publishing And Employee View

## Purpose

Generate professional payslips and publish them to employees.

## Payslip Contents

- Company name/logo.
- Employee name.
- Employee ID.
- Designation.
- Department.
- Month.
- Paid days.
- Earnings.
- Deductions.
- Employer contributions if enabled.
- Gross pay.
- Net pay.
- Amount in words.
- Bank masked.
- Notes.

## Payslip Templates

At least three visual layouts:

- Classic table.
- Modern compact.
- Detailed compliance.

## Publishing

Options:

- Publish immediately after payment.
- Publish manually.
- Email payslip.
- Employee portal download.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Payslip matches locked payroll snapshot.
- Published payslip cannot silently change.
- Employee can download PDF.
