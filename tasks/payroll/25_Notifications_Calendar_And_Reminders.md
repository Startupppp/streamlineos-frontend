# Notifications Calendar And Reminders

## Purpose

Keep payroll on schedule.

## Payroll Calendar

Events:

- Attendance cutoff.
- Reimbursement cutoff.
- Tax declaration cutoff.
- Payroll preview date.
- Approval deadline.
- Payment date.
- Payslip publish date.

## Notifications

Notify:

- Employees to submit declarations.
- Managers to approve claims.
- Payroll admins about exceptions.
- Finance approvers about pending approval.
- Employees when payslip is published.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Notifications link to the exact action.
- Admin can configure reminder timing.
- Missed deadlines appear in command center.
