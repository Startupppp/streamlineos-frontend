# Global Contractor And Multi Currency Payroll

## Purpose

Support remote employees, contractors, and multi-country teams.

## Worker Types

- Employee.
- Contractor.
- Consultant.
- Intern.
- EOR employee placeholder.

## Multi-Currency Fields

- Payroll currency.
- Worker payout currency.
- FX rate.
- FX source.
- Converted amount.
- Payment method.

## Contractor Payroll

Includes:

- Contract fee.
- Invoice number.
- Withholding.
- Payment advice.
- Tax form placeholder.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Contractor payroll does not require employee statutory components.
- Currency conversion is visible and auditable.
- Global payroll has compliance checklist per country.
