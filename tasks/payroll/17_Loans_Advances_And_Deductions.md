# Loans Advances And Deductions

## Purpose

Manage employee salary advances and loan recovery.

## Loan Fields

- Employee.
- Loan/advance amount.
- Reason.
- EMI amount.
- Total EMIs.
- Paid EMIs.
- Start month.
- Status.
- Approver.

## Workflow

- Employee/admin creates request.
- Approval.
- Disbursement.
- EMI recovery in payroll.
- Closure.

## Deduction Controls

- Skip EMI.
- Extra recovery.
- Foreclosure.
- Manual adjustment.

## Acceptance Criteria

- EMI appears in payroll automatically.
- Loan balance is visible to employee where enabled.
- Loan changes are audited.
