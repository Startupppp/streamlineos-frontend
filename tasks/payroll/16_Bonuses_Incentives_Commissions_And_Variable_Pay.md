# Bonuses Incentives Commissions And Variable Pay

## Purpose

Support variable earnings.

## Types

- Performance bonus.
- Joining bonus.
- Retention bonus.
- Sales commission.
- Incentive.
- Referral bonus.
- Spot award.
- Adjustment.

## Sales Incentive Integration

Inputs:

- Sales rep.
- Client/account.
- Revenue.
- Incentive rate.
- Approved amount.
- Payroll month.

## Approval

Variable pay can require:

- Manager approval.
- Sales head approval.
- Finance approval.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Variable pay is visible before payroll approval.
- Bonus can be taxable/non-taxable based on configuration.
- Clawback adjustment is supported as a deduction.
