# Market Positioning And Buyer Personas

## Positioning

PayrollOS is a guided payroll command center for companies that want accurate salary processing without spreadsheets, manual payslip edits, or risky compliance gaps.

## Product Promise

Select a payroll template, turn on the rules you need, preview every payroll change, approve with confidence, pay employees, and give every employee a clean self-service payroll experience.

## Buyers

### Founder / Owner

Needs:

- Simple setup.
- Confidence before payment.
- Clean costs.
- No compliance surprises.

Wins:

- Template-based payroll launch.
- Clear monthly payroll total.
- Exception warnings.
- Bank transfer and payslip readiness.

### HR / Payroll Admin

Needs:

- Salary structures.
- Attendance/LOP sync.
- Reimbursements.
- Bonuses.
- Loans.
- Tax declarations.
- Payslips.
- Reports.

Wins:

- Payroll checklist.
- Bulk employee review.
- Exception queue.
- One-click payslip generation.

### Finance Head

Needs:

- Approval control.
- Bank payout files.
- Audit trail.
- Accounting reports.
- Cost center allocation.

Wins:

- Payroll variance report.
- Approval chain.
- Journal export.
- Department/cost-center reports.

### Employee

Needs:

- Payslips.
- Tax declarations.
- Reimbursement status.
- Loan balance.
- Bank detail update.
- Salary breakdown.

Wins:

- Employee payroll portal.
- Download payslips.
- Transparent deductions.
- Clear status.

### Contractor / Consultant

Needs:

- Invoice/consulting payout.
- TDS/withholding if applicable.
- Payment history.

Wins:

- Contractor pay template.
- Separate employee/contractor payroll logic.
