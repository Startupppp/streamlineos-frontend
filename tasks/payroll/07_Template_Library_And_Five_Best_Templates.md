# Template Library And Five Best Templates

## Purpose

Provide prebuilt payroll templates that owners can select, preview, and customize.

## Template 1: Indian Standard Payroll

Best for:

- Indian companies with regular salaried employees.

Includes:

- Basic.
- HRA.
- Special allowance.
- PF.
- Professional tax.
- TDS.
- LOP.
- Reimbursements.
- Payslip.

Default toggles:

- PF on.
- Professional tax on.
- TDS on.
- Attendance LOP on.
- Bank payout on.

## Template 2: Indian Startup Flexible Payroll

Best for:

- Startups with mixed salary components and reimbursements.

Includes:

- Basic.
- HRA.
- Special allowance.
- Internet allowance.
- Travel allowance.
- Meal allowance.
- Flexible reimbursements.
- Bonus.

Default toggles:

- Reimbursements on.
- Bonus on.
- PF optional.
- Professional tax on.
- Employee declarations on.

## Template 3: Contractor / Consultant Payroll

Best for:

- Consultants, freelancers, advisors, and retainers.

Includes:

- Contract fee.
- TDS/withholding.
- Invoice reference.
- Payment schedule.
- Contractor payslip/payment advice.

Default toggles:

- Contractor mode on.
- Attendance off.
- Reimbursements optional.
- Bank payout on.

## Template 4: Sales Incentive Payroll

Best for:

- Sales teams with commissions and performance incentives.

Includes:

- Fixed salary.
- Variable incentive.
- Commission rate.
- Target achievement.
- Bonus approval.
- Clawback adjustment.

Default toggles:

- Incentives on.
- Manager approval on.
- Payroll variance warnings on.
- Sales revenue import on.

## Template 5: Global Remote Payroll

Best for:

- Remote teams across countries or currencies.

Includes:

- Multi-currency pay.
- Contractor vs employee flag.
- Country-specific notes.
- Payment method.
- Compliance checklist placeholder.

Default toggles:

- Multi-currency on.
- Contractor support on.
- Country compliance checklist on.
- Global payment report on.

## Additional Templates

- Hourly / Timesheet Payroll.
- Manufacturing / Shift Payroll.
- Staffing Payroll.
- Executive Payroll.

## Acceptance Criteria

- At least five templates ship.
- Owner can duplicate a template.
- Owner can preview component math before applying.
- Template selection writes a payroll policy version.
