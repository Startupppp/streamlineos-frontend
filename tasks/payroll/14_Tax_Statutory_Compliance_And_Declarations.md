# Tax Statutory Compliance And Declarations

## Purpose

Manage tax and statutory payroll settings.

## India-Focused Statutory Toggles

- PF.
- ESI.
- Professional tax.
- TDS/income tax.
- Gratuity placeholder.
- Labour welfare fund placeholder.

## Employee Declarations

Employees can submit:

- Tax regime selection.
- Investment declarations.
- Rent/HRA declaration.
- Previous employment income.
- Tax proof documents.

## Admin Controls

- Declaration window.
- Proof approval.
- Tax lock date.
- Override reason.
- Tax report export.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Tax rules are versioned.
- Employee declarations affect payroll only after approval if configured.
- Admin can export tax-ready reports.
