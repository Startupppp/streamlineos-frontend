# Reports, Dashboards And Finance Analytics

## Existing Baseline
- Trial balance
- Profit and loss
- Balance sheet
- Cash flow
- Aged receivables
- Aged payables
- Customer/vendor ledger

## Add Reports
- General ledger
- Account transactions
- Customer statement
- Vendor statement
- Sales by customer
- Sales by product/service
- Expense by category
- Tax summary
- Budget vs actual
- Project profitability
- Department profitability
- Cash runway
- Burn rate
- Working capital

## Finance Dashboard
Cards:
- Cash balance
- Revenue this month
- Expenses this month
- Net profit
- AR overdue
- AP due next 7 days
- Tax payable
- Burn rate
- Runway
- Reconciliation gaps

## Drill-Down Rule
Every dashboard number must drill down to transactions.

## Export
Support:
- CSV
- XLSX later
- PDF later

## Acceptance Criteria
- Reports tie to ledger.
- Date filters work.
- Reports respect permissions.
- Dashboard numbers are explainable through drill-down.
