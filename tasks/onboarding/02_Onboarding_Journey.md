# StreamlineOS Product Bible

# Customer Onboarding Platform

# 02_Onboarding_Journey.md

## Purpose

Define the complete onboarding journey from account creation to first business success.

---

# Design Principles

- Time to First Value under 5 minutes
- AI-first experience
- Zero empty states
- Progressive disclosure
- Skippable where appropriate
- Resume anytime

---

# Journey Overview

1. Account Created
2. Welcome
3. Business Goals
4. Industry Selection
5. Company Profile
6. Workspace Generation
7. App Recommendations
8. Team Invitations
9. Integrations
10. Data Import
11. AI Personalization
12. Dashboard Ready
13. First Success Checklist

---

# Step 1 — Welcome

Display:

- Welcome message
- Estimated setup time (3–5 min)
- Progress indicator
- "Start Setup" button

Offer:
- Skip for now
- Resume later

---

# Step 2 — Business Goals

Question:

"What do you want to achieve?"

Allow multiple selections:

- Grow Sales
- Manage Employees
- Manage Inventory
- Finance & Accounting
- Customer Support
- Projects
- AI Automation
- Build Everything

These choices influence recommended apps.

---

# Step 3 — Industry

Examples:

- IT Services
- Agency
- Retail
- Manufacturing
- Healthcare
- Education
- Construction
- Real Estate
- Restaurant
- Logistics

Load industry-specific templates.

---

# Step 4 — Company Profile

Collect only essentials:

- Company Name
- Team Size
- Country
- Time Zone
- Currency

Everything else can be configured later.

---

# Step 5 — AI Workspace Generation

Show an animated progress screen.

AI creates:

- Organization
- Departments
- Teams
- Roles
- Sample dashboard
- Sample workflows
- Recommended apps

Never show a blank workspace.

---

# Step 6 — Recommended Apps

Display recommendations:

- Sales Suite
- People Suite
- Operations Suite
- Finance Suite
- Helpdesk

Allow:

- Install
- Skip
- Learn More

---

# Step 7 — Invite Team

Invite by email.

Support:

- Individual invites
- CSV upload
- Skip

---

# Step 8 — Connect Tools

Offer optional integrations:

- Google Workspace
- Microsoft 365
- Gmail
- Outlook
- Slack
- WhatsApp
- Calendar

---

# Step 9 — Import Data

Supported:

- CSV
- Excel

Import:

- Customers
- Employees
- Inventory
- Projects

Show progress and validation.

---

# Step 10 — AI Personalization

Ask:

"Would you like AI to customize your workspace?"

Generate:

- Dashboards
- Pipelines
- Workflows
- Reports
- KPIs

---

# Step 11 — Workspace Ready

Celebrate completion.

Display:

- Workspace summary
- Installed apps
- Team members
- Next recommended actions

Primary CTA:

Enter Workspace

---

# First Success Checklist

- Invite first teammate
- Create first record
- Connect email
- Complete profile
- Try AI Assistant

Completion unlocks achievement badges.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

- Completion in under 5 minutes
- Personalized experience
- AI-generated workspace
- Resume supported
- Production ready
