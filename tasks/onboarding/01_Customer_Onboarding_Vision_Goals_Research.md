# StreamlineOS Product Bible

# Customer Onboarding Platform

# 01_Vision_Goals_Research.md

## Purpose

The Customer Onboarding Platform transforms a new customer from account creation into a fully configured, production-ready workspace in under five minutes.

---

# Vision

Build the world's best AI-powered onboarding experience for business software.

---

# Mission

Provide an onboarding experience that:

- Understands the customer's business
- Recommends the right apps
- Creates the workspace automatically
- Imports data
- Invites teammates
- Guides users to first value

---

# Product Principles

- AI First
- Zero Empty States
- Outcome Driven
- Progressive Disclosure
- Enterprise Ready

---

# Customer Personas

- Founder
- Small Business Owner
- HR Manager
- Sales Manager
- Operations Manager
- Enterprise Admin

---

# Core Responsibilities

- AI onboarding assistant
- Workspace generation
- Industry templates
- Module recommendations
- Team invitations
- Guided setup
- Data import
- Integrations
- Activation tracking

---

# Success Metrics

- Time to First Value < 5 min
- Workspace setup completion > 90%
- Trial to Paid conversion improvement
- Team invitation rate
- First-week retention
- Module adoption

---

# Guiding Principle

Never show an empty workspace.

Every customer should immediately see a business-ready environment tailored to their industry.

---

# Definition of Done

Customers can create, personalize, configure, and begin using StreamlineOS without implementation assistance.
