## What Is StreamlineOS?

StreamlineOS is an **all-in-one enterprise SaaS platform** that unifies every operational function of your business — HR, Payroll, Projects, CRM, Support, Inventory, Accounting, and more — under a single roof, with a single login, and a single source of truth.

Built for **multi-tenant, multi-org enterprises**, it is:

- **Role-aware** — every user sees exactly what they're permitted to see, nothing more.
- **AI-powered** — an assistant baked into every module, not bolted on.
- **Real-time** — live chat, notifications, and collaboration across every team.
- **Global-ready** — statutory compliance for India, US, UK, UAE, Singapore, and Australia built in.
