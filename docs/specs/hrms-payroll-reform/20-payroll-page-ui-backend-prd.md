**Status:** Target-state specification for 25 canonical Payroll pages.  
**Scope and tier:** [PRD 07](./07-pages-navigation-roles-prd.md). **Authorization:** [PRD 07a](./07a-page-permission-manifest-prd.md), [Platform PRD 06](./06-platform-intelligence-compliance-prd.md) and [Payroll PRDs 05–05c](./05-india-payroll-prd.md). **Common state and accessibility contract:** [PRD 18](./18-self-service-page-ui-backend-prd.md#contract-applied-to-every-page-below).

## Module-level RBAC and payment safety

`Payroll` is an independent module entitlement. It never follows automatically from HRMS enablement, an HR administrator role, a manager role, or self-service. A Payroll operator requires the Payroll module, the exact stage permission, authorized pay group/entity, object status and field-purpose grant. A Payroll-only actor receives only the Core HR Employment Facts projection needed for payroll eligibility; that role cannot browse general HRMS records, cases, performance or documents. Worker self-pay and manager pay-readiness are narrower surfaces, with separate grants and no operator access. Backend controllers, internal jobs, exports and AskOS tools enforce this independently of the sidebar. Funding, bank changes, calculation overrides, approval, payout release, statutory submission and accounting acknowledgment have incompatible-role checks and cannot self-approve. Unknown external outcomes stop retry until enquiry/reconciliation. Generated artifacts never claim filed, paid or acknowledged status.

Every list and detail uses the common page contract from PRD 18. Every financial amount uses exact decimal/integer minor units with explicit currency and rounding policy. Business dates use the employer/pay-group calendar and effective rule version. Frozen inputs, calculated results, issued documents and closed runs preserve immutable generations; a later change creates a linked correction. Every action returns a receipt, version and visible recovery owner. The India rules and official forms are versioned by applicable period and reviewed by a named specialist; the UI does not hardcode rates or statutory claims.

## Completion rule

All 25 pages require their stage-specific entitlement, permission and DataScope tests for Payroll-only, HRMS-only, both and neither actors. Each page must demonstrate typed error/partial states, negative tests before success, accessibility and responsive evidence. Paid, published, filed and reconciled are independent facts. P1 contractor/provider work remains disabled until its own evidence passes. The [execution ledger](./architecture-review/execution-ledger.json) records architecture acceptance; [PRD 06a](./06a-operations-release-evidence-prd.md) owns launch gates.
