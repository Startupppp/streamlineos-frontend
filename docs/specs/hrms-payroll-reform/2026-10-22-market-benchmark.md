## 2. India tax transition: government-verified requirements

The following points are jurisdiction-specific and should be reviewed by payroll/legal owners before shipping a country pack:

1. The [Income-tax Act, 2025 commenced on 1 April 2026](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/income-tax-act-2025-comes-into-force-from-1st-april-2026). The department's [transition FAQ, Q6.22–6.24](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/81799/11848482/FAQs-on-Interplay-and-Transition.pdf) says salary TDS follows the **salary payment date** across this boundary, and the TY 2026–27 TDS projection resets from 1 April 2026 under section 392(1). For non-salary TDS, Q6.1 uses the earlier credit/payment trigger. A rule selector based only on salary period or certificate generation date is insufficient.
2. The [same transition FAQ, Q6.10–6.14](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/81799/11848482/FAQs-on-Interplay-and-Transition.pdf) says Q4 FY 2025–26 salary TDS remains Form 24Q even when filed in May 2026; TY 2026–27 salary TDS uses Form 138. Non-salary resident Form 26Q becomes Form 140. **Corrections for old periods retain old Act forms/formats after 1 April 2026.** See also the department's [Form 138](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/form-138-faqs) and [Form 140](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/form-140-faqs) guidance.
3. The [transition FAQ, Q6.15](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/81799/11848482/FAQs-on-Interplay-and-Transition.pdf) says **FY 2025–26 salary gets Form 16, due 15 June 2026**, while **TY 2026–27 salary gets Form 130, due 15 June 2027**. The governing period is decisive even when publication happens later.
4. The department's [Form 130 FAQ, questions 6–9](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/form-130-faqs) says Form 130 comes from processed Form 138 statements, must be **downloaded from TRACES**, and then signed and issued. An employer-created offline certificate is not a valid Form 130. Corrections require revised Form 138 processing followed by a corrected TRACES certificate. Therefore the PRD must distinguish internal tax worksheet/preparation from legal certificate retrieval and issue; never claim internal PDF generation equals a valid Form 130.
5. The official [EPFO ECR/payment FAQ](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Circulars/Y2020-2021/ECR_PaymentprocessFAQs.pdf) distinguishes ECR information creation and contribution payment. Its [2025 ECR revamp circular](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Circulars/Y2025-2026/ECRRevamp_26092025.pdf) shows formats can change, so the pack must version official templates and keep generated, uploaded, accepted, and paid as distinct states.

### Existing PRD wording to tighten

- `05a-india-payroll-statutory-prd.md` **FR-STAT-028** is directionally correct on period/version selection, but should explicitly separate the salary TDS **payment-date trigger** from the statement/certificate's governing period and correction framework.
- `05a` **FR-STAT-027**, **FR-STAT-028**, and its acceptance criteria should specify: prepare and reconcile Form 138 data; record filing/processing; retrieve government-issued Form 130 from TRACES; sign, publish, and retain retrieval evidence. Internal preview or worksheet must be labelled non-statutory.
- `05-india-payroll-prd.md` references Form 130/legacy Form 16. Add the FY 2025–26 vs. TY 2026–27 example above so implementers do not choose a form by issuance date.
- Revalidate schemas, rate tables, due dates, PT/LWF state rules, and provider capabilities immediately before launch. Neither this note nor vendor pages provide a complete statutory ruleset.

## 3. Open evidence before claiming competitive leadership

- Verify **plan, country, native/connected/partner/managed model, and current availability** with vendor documentation or demos for any cell used in sales or launch copy.
- Run task-based UX studies with HR, payroll, employees, managers, and finance across the intended company sizes. A marketing page cannot establish time-to-complete or error recovery quality.
- Validate India payroll fixtures and forms with payroll counsel/operators against the effective notification and authority portal; retain signed rule versions and acceptance evidence.
- Prioritize by target geography, workforce type, and interviews. This research is an evidence base, not a user-validated roadmap.
