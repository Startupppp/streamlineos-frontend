## 📋 **Device Management - NEW Feature Request**

### **What to Track:**

#### **1. Device Information:**
- Asset tag (e.g., VCV-LAP-001)
- Type (Laptop, Desktop, Mobile, Monitor, etc.)
- Brand & Model (Dell XPS 15, MacBook Pro M3)
- Specifications (16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, etc.)
- Serial number

#### **2. Purchase Details:**
- Purchase date & price
- Vendor (Amazon, Dell India)
- Warranty expiry date
- Invoice number

#### **3. Assignment Tracking:**
- Who has it currently
- When assigned
- Who assigned it
- Full assignment history (all previous users)
- Return condition

#### **4. Maintenance:**
- Last service date
- Repair history
- Service costs
- Condition (New, Good, Fair, Damaged)

#### **5. Status:**
- Available (in inventory)
- Assigned (with employee)
- In Repair (being serviced)
- Retired (out of service)
- Lost/Stolen

### **Key Features:**

1. ✅ **Device Registry** - List all company devices
2. ✅ **Assign to Employee** - With email notification
3. ✅ **Return from Employee** - Track condition
4. ✅ **Assignment History** - Who had what when
5. ✅ **Warranty Alerts** - Email before expiry
6. ✅ **Maintenance Log** - Track repairs & costs
7. ✅ **Reports** - Device utilization, costs, age analysis
8. ✅ **Employee View** - See their assigned devices

### **Email Notifications:**
- Device assigned to you
- Device return reminder
- Warranty expiring soon
- Maintenance scheduled
