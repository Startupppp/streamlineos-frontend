## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Invoice headers and line items are normalized and immutable after issue. — **normalized and enforced in two places; one journal entry short of done.** `invoice-snapshot.ts:35-75` / `:77-101` are separate, normalized tables. At the service layer, `invoice-snapshot.service.ts:181-242` is the only update path and it writes nothing but `status` + a timestamp — asserted at `invoice-snapshot.service.spec.ts:387-400`, which reads the update's key set and fails if any amount, currency or number appears in it. At the database layer, **new migration `0565_billing_invoice_snapshot_immutability.sql`** puts `BEFORE UPDATE` triggers on `billing_invoice_snapshots`, `billing_invoice_line_snapshots`, `billing_credit_notes` and `billing_credit_note_lines`, guarding every snapshotted field of a non-DRAFT row; `invoice-snapshot.service.spec.ts:499-543` reads the migration and pins its shape. **Blocker:** S2 may not edit `migrations/meta/_journal.json`, and a migration absent from the journal never runs while `db:migrate` reports success. The exact entry is in [`../../lane-requests/s2.md`](../../lane-requests/s2.md).
- [x] Seller/buyer identity, addresses, tax registrations, place of supply, rates and tax behavior are snapshotted. — `invoice-snapshot.ts:43-51` holds the columns; `invoice-snapshot.service.ts:113-142` writes every one of them at issue, and `invoice-snapshot.service.spec.ts:153-171` asserts both parties, both sets of tax registrations, the place of supply and the tax behaviour land on the row. Per-line rates are `taxRateBps` (`:89`), written at `:149-166`.
- [x] Amounts use integer minor units and every row carries ISO currency. — `invoice-snapshot.ts:55-57`, `:87`. `money-rounding.ts` computes every amount in `bigint` and refuses anything outside the `integer` column's range; a fractional unit amount is refused rather than rounded (`invoice-snapshot.service.spec.ts:307-316`). The currency is validated against `/^[A-Z]{3}$/` at `invoice-snapshot.service.ts:89-90` and stamped on the header **and every line** (`spec:190-212`).
- [x] FX conversions store rate, source and timestamp; historical invoices never use a new rate. — `invoice-snapshot.ts:52-54` written at `invoice-snapshot.service.ts:129-131` and asserted at `invoice-snapshot.service.spec.ts:173-188`. "Never uses a new rate" is what the immutability guard buys: `fx_rate_micro`, `fx_rate_source` and `fx_rate_captured_at` are all in migration `0565`'s guarded set, so an issued invoice cannot be re-rated.
- [x] Rounding is deterministic at line and document level. — `invoice-pricing.ts:26-30` prices each line and `:33-42` (`priceDocument`) makes the document totals the **sum of the priced lines**, never a separately-rounded figure. `invoice-snapshot.service.spec.ts:243-271` runs all four rounding rules over lines chosen to round in both directions and asserts `documentTotal === Σ lineTotals` and `total === subtotal + tax` every time. The rule used is stored on the header (`invoice-snapshot.service.ts:135`) so the document recomputes identically.
- [x] Corrections use credit/debit notes and preserve the original. — `invoice-snapshot.service.ts:244-319` (`createCreditNote`) allocates its own number, points at `originalSnapshotId` and **issues no update against the original** — asserted at `invoice-snapshot.service.spec.ts:440-464`, which fails if any update is issued. The note takes its currency from the original, so it cannot drift (`spec:466-486`), a draft invoice cannot be credited, and voiding a paid invoice is refused and points at the credit note instead (`spec:402-410`).
- [x] Invoice numbering is transactionally serialized and auditable. — `invoice-numbering.ts:12-31` (`allocateDocumentNumber`, called at `invoice-snapshot.service.ts:102` and `:272`) takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('invoice:<orgId>:<prefix>:<year>', 0))` (`:6-9`) and then increments `billing_invoice_number_sequences.last_number` through an `ON CONFLICT … DO UPDATE … RETURNING`, so the number comes from a durable counter rather than a `COUNT(*)` that a delete or a concurrent insert could repeat. It **refuses rather than numbering an invoice from a sequence it could not read** (`invoice-snapshot.service.spec.ts:365-373`). Ordering, the sequence SQL, the per-year scope and prefix validation are asserted at `spec:328-384`.

**Correction to this ticket's premise (2026-08-27, S2):** the blocker recorded here was "migration 0492 is journalled but unapplied". **0492 was never relevant.** Its trigger `trg_invoice_immutability` is `BEFORE UPDATE ON invoices` — the organisation's own customer invoicing under accounting (root `CLAUDE.md` §8), a different table from `billing_invoice_snapshots`. Applying 0492 would not have satisfied criterion 1 by a single field. The real gap was that no trigger existed for the platform invoice snapshots at all, which `0565` now supplies.

**Design decision:** `0565` guards `UPDATE` only, never `DELETE`. An `ON DELETE CASCADE` from `organizations` has to be able to remove these rows for tenant erasure; an issued snapshot is protected from deletion instead by `billing_credit_notes.original_snapshot_id`, which is `ON DELETE RESTRICT`.

**Audit note (2026-08-26):** All seven schema tables/structures verified. No migration exists for any of these tables. The single hard blocker is the immutability trigger (migration 0492, journalled but unapplied) — without it criterion 1 is not enforced. All other criteria are satisfied structurally at schema level. *(Superseded: `0524` is journalled as `idx` 297; the 0492 premise is wrong, see the correction above; and the assembly service now exists.)*
